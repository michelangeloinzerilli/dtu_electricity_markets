# Renewables in Electricity Markets

Projects developed as part of the **Renewables in Electricity Markets** course at the **Technical University of Denmark (DTU)**.

The repository contains two assignments exploring electricity markets from both a **system** and a **market-participant** perspective, using mathematical optimization, stochastic modelling and electricity-market data.

## Assignment 1 – System Perspective

Electricity market modelling from the perspective of the power system and market operator.

The assignment develops and progressively extends market-clearing optimization models to analyze:

- Day-Ahead market clearing and price formation
- Social welfare and market participant revenues
- Multi-period operation and energy storage
- Nodal and zonal electricity markets
- Transmission congestion
- Balancing markets and imbalance settlement
- Reserve procurement
- Optimization and market equilibrium

The analysis combines market-clearing models with sensitivity studies to investigate how flexibility, network constraints and different market designs influence electricity prices and market outcomes.

## Assignment 2 – Stakeholder Perspective

Optimization of market participation strategies for renewable and flexible energy resources under uncertainty.

The assignment investigates:

- Stochastic Day-Ahead bidding of wind generation
- One-price and two-price balancing schemes
- Scenario generation and out-of-sample validation
- Risk-averse bidding using **Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR)**
- Participation of flexible loads in the **FCR-D UP** ancillary-service market
- Reserve bidding under probabilistic availability requirements
- **ALSO-X and CVaR** approaches for uncertainty management

The analysis focuses on the trade-offs between expected profitability, risk, reliability and market participation under uncertain renewable generation, electricity prices and flexible-load availability.
